## Design-specificity verdict

Pass. KAVY feels authored for private-markets underwriting through its workflow, terminology, cases, debt schedule, return bridge, sensitivity lab, and local-model trust language. It is not category-interchangeable. The main risk is that “PRIVATE MARKETS OS,” “MODEL TAPE,” “Atlas v2.4,” “RUN 042,” and “LIVE MODEL” sometimes become institutional theater instead of decision-support. The detector returned `[]`, so no automated integrity findings were reported.

## Priority issues

### [P1] 320px responsive overflow
At 320px, the document measured 367px wide. The returns waterfall/bar content retains a minimum width. Fix grid children with `min-width: 0`, constrain the waterfall, and allow internal scrolling only for truly wide tables.

### [P1] Muted/chart contrast below AA
Measured ratios include approximately 3.93:1 and 3.47:1 against the dark surface. Raise muted tokens and use the faintest colors only for decorative lines.

### [P2] Touch targets are too small
At 320px, sidebar controls were about 41x37px, workspace switcher 41x29px, and number inputs about 17px high. Preserve compact visuals but increase hit areas to at least 44px.

### [P2] Utility modal focus is incomplete
The modal focuses close on open and closes with Escape, but does not trap focus, restore focus to the trigger, or inert the background.

### [P2] Duplicate CSS token systems
`styles.css` contains an older `--kavy-*` layer and a later generic token layer, followed by override drift. Consolidate semantic tokens.

### [P2] First viewport is over-chromed
Header, save state, orientation note, model tape, market tape, ticker, and action row consume roughly 40% of desktop vertical space before assumptions begin. Combine status surfaces and progressively disclose advanced forecast inputs.

### [P2] Calculation freshness is ambiguous
The app says results update as you type while also presenting Recalculate model, Saved just now, LIVE MODEL, and Last run. Choose one calculation model and expose one freshness state.

### [P2] Output explanations need stronger traceability
Bridge colors, sensitivity axes, and radial IRR semantics need legends and selected-cell explanations. Put causal wording below the headline: e.g. “IRR increased because debt paydown contributed X.”

### [P2] Finance-specific error recovery is underdeveloped
Validate relationships such as terminal growth < WACC, debt service versus cash generation, negative equity, and hold-period mismatch; preserve the last valid model and show a direct fix.

### [P3] Placeholder controls weaken trust
Search, More, Open IC prep, and Notifications expose toast-only/no-op states. Implement them or label them unavailable/coming soon.

## Persona red flags

**Associate / VP underwriting:** Good cases, exports, bridge, sensitivity, and shortcut support. Friction comes from long vertical scanning, duplicated status labels, hidden jump shortcuts, and ambiguous recalculation behavior.

**First-timer / student / founder:** Orientation and helper copy help, but six inputs still feel like an exam; EBITDA, MOIC, IRR, and cash sweep remain unexplained at the point of use. Mobile shows only the beginning of the long assumptions flow.

## Minor observations

- 10px mono labels are coherent but small for prolonged spreadsheet-like work.
- Gold is used for actions, selection, positive values, and status emphasis; semantic separation would improve scanning.
- The radial IRR graphic implies a target/completion gauge without documenting its scale.
- Illustrative-data disclosure is not adjacent to every high-salience result.
- Icon-only mobile navigation depends on recognition and could use tooltips/accessible names.

## Questions to consider

1. Is the institutional-terminal framing helping underwriting, or asking users to role-play before they trust the math?
2. If results update as users type, why is Recalculate the dominant action?
3. Which single return number should an IC member trust first?
4. What would remain if every status badge and decorative telemetry item had to justify a decision?
